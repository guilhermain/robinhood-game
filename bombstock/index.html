The loop

You buy heroes with $BSTOCK and send up to ten into a mine. They walk, plant bombs and break chests on their own. Every chest pays a small amount in dollars. When you claim, those dollars become real tokenized stocks on Robinhood Chain: NVDA, GME, AMZN, MSTR, META, SPCX or USDG. The mining is counted by the server, so it keeps going after you close the tab.

Heroes

A hero costs 10 $BSTOCK; packs of 5 and 10 cost 50 and 100. No discount and no guaranteed rarity: every hero is rolled the same way, on-chain.

Energy and rest

A bomb costs 1 energy. Energy is Stamina × 50 and recharges 0.5 per minute, always. At zero the hero sits down and does not get up on his own: you send him back when you decide. Since recharge is the same for everyone, no hero plants more than 720 bombs a day.

The money

The game prints nothing. $BSTOCK trades on Pons with a 3% creator fee: 60% buys the seven stocks for the reward vault, 20% team, 10% burns $BSTOCK, 10% reserve. $BSTOCK spent inside the game goes to a separate vault.

Claiming

Mining runs in 24-hour epochs. At the end the server freezes what everyone earned and the prices, builds a Merkle tree and publishes its root on-chain. You claim with a proof; the contract pays only addresses in the tree. 3% withdrawal fee, $10 minimum. You receive real tokenized stocks.

What keeps it honest

  • Your browser cannot cheat. The server counts; chests broken from the console credit nothing.
  • The vault cannot be drained. It pays only what a published Merkle root says, once per address per epoch.
  • Rarity is rolled on-chain. Nobody picks who gets a Legendary.
  • One signature to sign in. Never a seed phrase; token approvals are for the exact amount only.
  • Bots gain nothing. Mining is server-side and every hero has the same daily ceiling.
