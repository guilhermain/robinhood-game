How it works

You buy heroes with $BSTOCK. You send up to ten of them into a mine. They walk around, plant bombs, and break chests. Every chest pays a small amount in dollars. When you claim, those dollars turn into real tokenized stocks on Robinhood Chain: NVDA, GME, AMZN, MSTR, META, SPCX, or USDG.

The heroes work on their own. You pick who goes down, which mine, and when they rest. The mining itself is counted by the server, not by your browser, so it keeps running when you close the tab.

Heroes and rarity

A hero costs 10 $BSTOCK. Packs of 1, 5 or 10 cost 10, 50 and 100. No discount, no guaranteed rarity: every hero in a pack is rolled the same way, and the roll happens on-chain.

Five attributes, each rolled inside the rarity's range: Power (damage per bomb), Stamina (energy reserve, ×50), Speed, Bombs (how many at once) and Range (blast reach). Heroes may also carry skills: Jail Breaker, Pierce Block, Save Battery, Fast Charge, Bomb Pass, Block Pass, Treasure Hunter.

The average hero pays for itself in about 30 days. Rare ones much faster, Common slower. That is the price of rolling: you are buying the chance, not the outcome.

Mines and chests

The Green Field is free and pays all seven stocks at random. The other six mines cost 10 $BSTOCK each and pay only their own stock. Every mine pays the same money for the same work. The only difference is which stock you receive.

Energy and rest

Each bomb costs 1 energy. Energy is Stamina × 50 and recharges at 0.5 per minute, working or resting. When it hits zero the hero sits down and stops. He does not get up on his own. You send him back when you decide he has rested enough.

Since the recharge is the same for everyone, no hero can plant more than 720 bombs a day. That is the ceiling, for every player.

Where the money comes from

The game does not print anything. Every dollar paid to a chest was first paid into the vault.

$BSTOCK trades on the Pons launchpad with a 3% creator fee. That fee funds the game: 60% buys the seven stocks and goes to the reward vault, 20% team, 10% burns $BSTOCK, 10% reserve. $BSTOCK spent inside the game (heroes, mines) goes to a separate vault.

This means payouts depend on trading volume. Thirty-day payback with 1,000 active heroes needs roughly $15,000 of daily $BSTOCK volume. The numbers live in a config table and will be tuned against real volume.

Claiming your stocks

Mining is counted in epochs of 24 hours. At the end of each epoch the server freezes what everyone earned and the stock prices, builds a Merkle tree, and publishes its root on-chain. You then claim with a proof; the contract only pays addresses that are in the tree. A 3% withdrawal fee applies. Minimum claim: $10.

Withdrawals pay in real tokenized stocks. You can hold them, sell them, or convert to $BSTOCK.

What keeps it honest

  • Your browser cannot cheat. Chests broken from the console credit nothing. The server counts.
  • The vault cannot be drained. It only pays what a published Merkle root says, once per address per epoch.
  • Rarity is rolled on-chain. The game cannot pick who gets a Legendary.
  • Signing in is one signature. Never a seed phrase, never an unlimited token approval: the game approves only the exact amount of each purchase.
  • Bots gain nothing. Mining runs server-side around the clock and every hero has the same daily ceiling, so automating a browser earns exactly what playing does.
